# Present but Missing: SEO notes

Page: https://thewarthens.com/faith/present-but-missing (short link: thewarthens.com/present)

## What is already done

- Search title (59 characters) and description (141 characters) written around "Bible lesson for men".
- Canonical address, Open Graph and Twitter tags, article dates, author and section.
- Structured data on the page: Breadcrumb, Article (author, publisher, free to read, audience), and FAQ.
- A plain-language opening paragraph and a visible "Common questions" section with seven answers. The FAQ data sent to search engines is the same text people see.
- Links to the page from the Faith Journey page (Writings section), the site footer, the site guide, the sitemap, and `public/llms.txt`.
- A blue-framed share thumbnail that is generated for this page.
- `/present` is a temporary (307) redirect, so the QR code can be repointed later without breaking anything.

## Searches this page is built to match

- present but missing men's Bible study
- Bible lesson for men on being present
- being present as a husband and father Bible
- Isaiah 58 men's lesson
- Matthew 23:23 justice, mercy, and faith for men
- men's group lesson with reflection questions

This is a narrow topic, so expect a steady trickle of the right people, not big traffic. Most visits will
come from your sessions, the QR code, and the link in messages.

## What only you can do

1. In Google Search Console, open URL Inspection, paste the page address, and choose Request indexing.
   Do the same in Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Share the link where people will click it: the Faith Journey video descriptions, the church or men's
   group page, and your social profiles. Links from real pages are the biggest ranking factor left.
3. After two to four weeks, check Search Console Performance for the page and see which searches bring
   people in. Tell me what shows up and I will tune the wording.

## Checks to re-run after changing the page

- Title under about 60 characters, description under about 160.
- One h1, FAQ answers match what the page does, no em dashes.
- Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) shows Article and FAQ with no errors.
