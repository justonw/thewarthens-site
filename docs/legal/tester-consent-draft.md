## Thank you for helping us test
We are testing The Warthens Resume Builder and its tools (tailoring a resume to a job, follow-up questions, interview practice, and application answers) with a small group before it opens to everyone.

## What you do
Use the tools the way you would for a real job search, or use made-up information if you prefer. **You never have to use real personal details.** Tell us what worked, what felt wrong, and what was inaccurate.

## What happens to your information
- To write your documents, the tools send what you enter to our AI provider (Anthropic) and return the result. We do not keep a copy of your resume or the job on our servers.
- **Optional sharing:** at the end of a build you may see a box asking to share your build with us. It is off unless you tick it. If you do, we show you the version we would keep, with personal details removed (such as your name, email, phone, address, and links), and you can edit it or cancel before anything is saved. **We cannot guarantee that every identifying detail will be removed**, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.
