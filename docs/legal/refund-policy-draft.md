## All sales are final
Packs of resume builds are digital and used immediately. Once you buy a pack, **all sales are final and we do not offer refunds.** Please use your 2 free builds first to see if the tools are right for you.

## Builds that do not work are given back
If a build fails, or a result never reaches you, we give that build back automatically. This is not a refund: you never use up a build for a document you did not receive. If you think a build was lost, email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the address you used at checkout.

## Billing mistakes
If you were charged twice for the same pack, or you see a charge you did not make, contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]** right away and we will fix it. Please contact us before disputing the charge with your bank, so we can resolve it quickly.

## Notes for you (not part of the policy)
1. **Show it before they pay.** A no-refund policy holds up best when it is clearly stated before purchase. Put "All sales are final. See our Refund Policy." right at the pack's buy button (the page already says "paid builds"), and link the policy from the footer and the terms. Some states require a no-refund policy to be posted clearly at the point of sale, and a clear notice is also your best evidence if a customer disputes a charge with their bank.
2. **Chargebacks still happen.** A bank can reverse a charge even under a no-refund policy. Keep the evidence Stripe asks for: the purchase record, that builds were delivered and used, and this policy. The failed-build handback already shows good faith.
3. **Refunds you may still choose to give.** "No refunds" is the policy, not a ban. You can still make a goodwill exception (a duplicate charge, a real problem you cannot fix). Stripe refunds still arrive through the same webhook, so keep the `charge.refunded` event switched on in Stripe so those and any chargebacks are reflected, including partner commission clawbacks.
4. **The partner agreement** should say commissions on refunded or charged-back sales are returned.
5. If the Job Search Pass or a larger bundle launches, add a line for it here and show the notice at that buy button too.
