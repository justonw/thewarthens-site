# Resume tools: 30-day plan (no new money needed)

Goal for the month: find out what makes people finish a resume and come back, and build the foundation for the bigger bets (accounts, a saved profile, partnerships). Everything here uses tools we already have (Netlify, Resend, Stripe, Anthropic) or free tools.

## What we protect
- Honesty: the tools never invent experience.
- Free-first: the keyword scanner stays free.
- Your voice: faith, family, and plain talk.

## Week 1: See what is happening
**Claude builds**
- A first-party funnel counter (no third-party tracking, no cost): visit, file added, job added, email entered, build finished, download, gap-coach question answered, return visit. Stored in Netlify Blobs, shown in a private owner page.
- A weekly summary email to the owner with the funnel numbers.

**Owner does**
- Confirm Google Search Console and Bing show the new pages. Request indexing for `/resume`, `/resume/keyword-matcher`, and the six new guides.
- Link `/resume` and `/quiz` from YouTube descriptions and pinned comments (done for the new video).

**Done when:** we can say how many visitors finished a resume last week and where the biggest drop is.

## Week 2: Make it worth coming back to
**Claude builds**
- A saved profile on the device (no account yet): the resume, the notes, and every answer from the gap coach are kept in the browser, so the next job takes one click. Clear "forget me" button.
- A "story bank" view: everything the person said they have done, in one list they can edit.

**Owner does**
- Try it on your own resume and tell us what felt confusing.

**Done when:** a second build for a different job takes under a minute.

## Week 3: Quality you can prove
**Claude builds**
- A bigger set of test resumes and postings (about 30 across fields) with automatic checks for: no invented facts, keyword coverage, formatting, no typos, sensible gap-coach questions.
- A report after each prompt change showing what got better or worse.

**Owner does**
- Share 5 real resumes (with names removed) from friends or past coaching clients, with permission, to test on.

**Done when:** we can change the AI instructions and know right away if quality dropped.

## Week 4: Get found and get partners
**Claude builds**
- 10 new pages that answer real searches (for example "resume for customer service," "resume keywords for sales"), each with the scanner and builder linked. Sourced facts only.
- A welcome email series (3 emails) for people who join the occasional-notes list.
- A one-page partner sheet for churches, colleges, and workforce programs: what the tools do, that they are free to start, and how a group can use them.

**Owner does**
- Contact 10 people who run job-readiness programs, church career ministries, or community colleges. Offer a free session or a link.
- Stripe: finish the LLC step so paid packs can go live.

**Done when:** at least 3 partners say yes to a pilot, and 10 new pages are indexed.

## Measures to watch
- Visitors who finish a resume (target: up each week)
- Second build within 7 days (return rate)
- Gap-coach questions answered per build
- Search impressions and clicks (Search Console)
- Partner conversations started

## Later (needs money or a team)
- Accounts and cloud-saved profiles
- Browser extension for LinkedIn and job sites
- Full job tracker with reminders and follow-up emails
- Interview practice from the posting and the person's own stories
- Security review and formal privacy program
- Paid human review and coaching marketplace
