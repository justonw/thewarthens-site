# Source of Life: launch playbook

Page: https://thewarthens.com/source-of-life (short link: https://thewarthens.com/source)
Leader's guide: https://thewarthens.com/source-of-life/leader-guide

Everything below is a draft in Juston's voice. Edit freely. House rules: no em dashes, lead with the question and not the theology, and frame the page as a study held with conviction and open to other views.

## 1. The one line to lead with

> Is everything good from God and everything bad from the devil? Take this 2-minute check, then tell me what you put: thewarthens.com/source

Variations:
- "A pulpit line stuck with me: whatever good happens is God, whatever bad happens is the devil. I wanted to test it against Scripture. thewarthens.com/source"
- "Good is not always God. “Bad” is not always the devil. And you can still praise Him anyway. thewarthens.com/source"

## 2. Before the public launch (this week)

1. Send the link to 3 to 5 trusted people, including a pastor or elder you respect.
2. Ask each: "What did you think this page was about?" and "Is there anything you would push back on?"
3. Fix what they find, then launch.

## 3. Video: "Is Everything Good From God and Everything Bad From the Devil?"

Title options:
- Is Everything Good From God and Everything Bad From the Devil? (search friendly)
- Good Is Not Always God. “Bad” Is Not Always the Devil.

Outline (about 12 to 18 minutes). Use teach mode on the page so one verse fills the screen at a time:
1. Hook (0:00): the pulpit line, "whatever good happens is God, whatever bad happens is the devil". Ask: "Is that true?"
2. The quick check (1:00): take the check on screen. Say your own answers out loud.
3. The foundation (3:00): a cracked foundation versus a solid one. Matthew 7:24-27, 1 Corinthians 3:11.
4. Act I, Know Him (5:00): Romans 11:36, John 1:1-3, Colossians 2:10.
5. Act II, See Him (8:00): the Hebrew note on "evil", Isaiah 45:7, Job 1:12, Genesis 50:20, James 1:13. Say plainly that thoughtful Christians differ here.
6. Act III, Come to Him (13:00): Proverbs 3:6, James 4:7 (submit first, then resist), Romans 8:28-29.
7. Close (16:00): the prayer from the page. Point to thewarthens.com/source and the "Hurting right now?" button.

## 4. Shorts, Reels, and TikToks

Start each on a question, end on "link in bio". Ideas:
1. "Who do you think is behind this?" Read one card from the check, then ask for answers.
2. "Good is not always God." Why it matters, with James 1:17 against Matthew 4:8-9.
3. "“Bad” is not always the devil." Luke 13:1-5 and John 9:3.
4. "Submit first, then resist." James 4:7.
5. "What the word ‘evil’ means in Isaiah 45:7." The Hebrew note.
6. "A prayer if you are hurting right now." Read the prayer from the page.

Use the 9:16 "See full video" overlay style with the text "Take the 2-minute check".

Email to a pastor or small-group leader:

>

I hold it as my conviction and not the whole picture.

## 6. Small groups and churches

Send the leader's guide: thewarthens.com/source-of-life/leader-guide. It prints cleanly and includes the plan, the verses, discussion questions, and what to do if someone is hurting.

## 7. Four-week plan

- Week 1: trusted readers, fixes, the short link everywhere, Search Console.
- Week 2: publish the video, pin the comment, post the carousel and story poll.
- Week 3: Shorts from the video, send the leader's guide to 5 to 10 group leaders.
- Week 4: read the feedback and replies, then make a second pass on the page.
